# Owner Manual — Range, Riding Modes and Charging

## Specifications

| Model | Battery | Certified range | Top speed | Kerb weight | Rated payload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC-F50 | 5.0 kWh | 265 km | 115 km/h | 129 kg | 150 kg |
| SC-F45 | 4.5 kWh | 236 km | 90 km/h | 129 kg | 150 kg |
| SC-F50G1 | 5.0 kWh | 248 km | 105 km/h | 137 kg | 150 kg |

## Riding modes

The dashboard shows up to six named modes. For service records they
are grouped into three:

- **eco** — lowest power, longest range.
- **city** — balanced for daily riding.
- **sport** — full power; range falls noticeably.

## Why real-world range is lower than certified range

Certified range is measured on a standard test cycle at low speed
with a light rider. Expect 65–80% of it in daily use. Range falls
further with:

- sport mode or sustained high speed;
- load above the rated payload;
- very hot or very cold weather;
- low tyre pressure.

## Charging

| Charger | 0–80% | 0–100% |
|---|---|---|
| Standard 750 W home charger | about 4 h | about 8 h |

Charge in the shade in hot weather. Above 45 °C pack temperature,
charging current is reduced automatically (code ERR_302).
