# KB — "My range is lower than advertised"

Range complaints are the most common service contact. Work through
these checks in order before replacing any part.

1. **Ride mode.** Sonic uses nearly twice the energy per kilometre of
   Eco X. A rider who lives in Air or Sonic will see far less than the
   certified range.
2. **Load.** Compare recorded payload with the 150 kg rated payload.
   See SB-114.
3. **Cell health.** Below 90% points to pack wear, most often the
   removable pack. See SB-121.
4. **Charging.** If the scooter is not full in the morning, check
   charging power. See SB-140.
5. **Tyre pressure and brakes.** A dragging brake or soft tyre adds
   rolling resistance.

If all checks are normal and range is still below 65% of the
certified figure, raise a field report with the last 14 days of
telemetry attached.
