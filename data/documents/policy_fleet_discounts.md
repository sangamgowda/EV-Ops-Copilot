# Policy — Fleet and Partner Pricing

| Deal type | Minimum units | Maximum discount |
|---|---|---|
| Retail (showroom or online) | 1 | 3% |
| Partner (rental, subscription) | 10 | 7% |
| Fleet (delivery, logistics) | 25 | 10% |

Discounts above these limits need regional sales head approval.
Fleet deals must record the fleet size on the transaction.
