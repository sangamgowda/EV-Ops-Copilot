# Sales Update — Q3 2026 mid-quarter

## Headline

The south has pulled further ahead of the other regions this quarter.
The main reason is the EVX Max, which launched in the south on
1 June 2026 and reached the west, north and east only on 15 August.

## Why the south outperformed

- **Ten weeks of exclusive EVX Max supply.** From June to mid-August the
  EVX Max could only be bought in the south. Buyers who wanted the
  115 km/h model and the 6.0 kWh battery had nowhere else to go, and
  south showrooms converted pent-up demand from the launch campaign.
- **The EVX Max sold on top of, not instead of, the other models.**
  EVX Standard and EVX Pro volumes in the south held steady; the EVX Max added
  to them.
- **Fleet renewals.** Two delivery operators in Bengaluru and
  Hyderabad renewed on fleet pricing (see the fleet pricing policy).

## Other regions

The west, north and east were roughly flat on Q2 until the EVX Max
arrived in mid-August. Early EVX Max orders there are encouraging, but
the full effect will show in Q4.

## What to watch

- Whether the other regions catch up now that the EVX Max is on sale
  everywhere.
- EVX Max share of the model mix, which was about 30% of south sales
  after launch.
