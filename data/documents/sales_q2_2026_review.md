# Sales Review — Q2 2026

## Headline

Q2 volumes held above 1,100 units a month after the record March.
The brand has ranked in the national top ten for electric scooters
every month since February.

## What drove the south

- More showrooms: the south network grew from 38 to 52 outlets
  between January and June.
- Fleet deals with two last-mile delivery operators in Bengaluru and
  Hyderabad accounted for about 12% of south volume.
- A zero-cost financing offer through two finance partners lifted
  showroom conversion.

## Other regions

The west grew from a small base after new outlets opened in Pune.
The north and east remain early-stage, with fewer than five outlets
each.

## Model mix

The 5 kWh flagship was the best seller. The 4.5 kWh variant gained
share among first-time buyers because of its lower price.
